How to Plan a Stress-Free Family Vacation

Campfly Editorial
Sep 02, 2026
7 min read

Family vacations create lifelong memories, but the planning phase can often induce anxiety. By following a few golden rules, you can ensure a smooth, enjoyable trip for both parents and kids.

1. Choose the Right Destination

Opt for destinations that offer a mix of relaxation for parents and activities for children. Beach resorts with kids' clubs, theme park cities, or nature retreats with easy trails are ideal. Avoid destinations that require extensive moving around or very long transits.

2. Don't Overpack the Itinerary

One major activity a day is plenty. Build in "downtime" every afternoon. Kids get tired, and tired kids equal cranky kids. Having free time by the pool or taking a nap can save the whole day.

"Flexibility is your best friend on a family trip. Be prepared to ditch the itinerary if the kids are exhausted."

3. Pack Smartly

Pack a dedicated "entertainment bag" for transit—filled with new toys, coloring books, an iPad with pre-downloaded movies, and plenty of snacks. Hungry kids are unhappy travelers, so keep snacks accessible at all times.

4. Pre-book Everything

From airport transfers to attraction tickets, pre-book as much as possible. Standing in a 45-minute queue to buy tickets with an impatient toddler is a recipe for disaster.

5. Let the Kids Have a Say

If your children are old enough, let them pick one activity on the trip. Whether it's visiting an aquarium or choosing where to have ice cream, giving them autonomy keeps them engaged and excited.
